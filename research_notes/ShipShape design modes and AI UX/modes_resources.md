## Startup mode: formation & early-stage paperwork (Delaware, 83(b), BOI, EIN, SAFE, 409A, foreign qualification, Form D)

### Takeaway
Startup mode should be built around these items:
- a Delaware franchise-tax and annual-report calendar (corporations due March 1; LLCs due June 1, with the LLC tax rising to $400 on the June 2027 bill under HB 400);
- a 30-day 83(b) clock, now fileable online with IRS Form 15620 via ID.me;
- Form D 15 days after first sale on EDGAR (EDGAR Next credentials);
- the fact that BOI/CTA reporting is permanently gone for domestic companies under FinCEN's August 2026 final rule. This item has changed most recently, and older content is now wrong.

### Cited Findings
**Delaware Division of Corporations (primary source)**
- Domestic corporation annual reports and franchise taxes are due **March 1** for the prior year and must be filed online. — [Delaware Division of Corporations, Pay Taxes](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/)
- Late penalty is **$200 plus 1.5% monthly interest** on tax and penalty. — [corp.delaware.gov](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/)
- Annual report fee is $50 for non-exempt and $25 for exempt domestic corporations (since 9/1/2019). — [corp.delaware.gov](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/)
- Minimum tax is **$175 under the Authorized Shares Method** and **$400 under the Assumed Par Value Capital Method**. Maximum is $200,000, or $250,000 for Large Corporate Filers. — [corp.delaware.gov](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/)
- Foreign corporations (registered in DE) file annual reports **June 30** with a $250 fee and a $250 late penalty. A "nature of business" entry is now required on all domestic annual reports. — [corp.delaware.gov](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/)
- **Recently changed:** Delaware HB 400 was signed May 21, 2026. It raises the annual tax for LLCs, LPs and GPs from **$300 to $400**, retroactive to Jan 1, 2026. Because the tax is billed in arrears, the June 1, 2026 payment was still $300 and the **$400 first appears on the June 1, 2027 payment**. LLP/LLLP per-partner tax goes from $200 to $300, and series LLC per-series tax from $75 to $100. Most other filing-fee changes took effect Aug 1, 2026. Corporate franchise tax is not affected. — [Bloomberg Tax](https://news.bloombergtax.com/daily-tax-report-state/delaware-increases-secretary-of-state-entity-fees-annual-taxes); [Shumaker client alert](https://www.shumaker.com/insight/client-alert-how-delawares-new-fee-and-tax-increases-may-affect-your-business-entities/); [Withum](https://www.withum.com/resources/delaware-businesses-face-increased-annual-taxes-and-filing-costs/); [InCorp on Aug 1, 2026 fee changes](https://www.incorp.com/resources/knowledge-base/delaware-business-filing-fee-increase)
- Delaware LLCs file no annual report, only the flat tax due June 1. Missing it costs a $200 penalty plus 1.5%/month and loss of good standing. — [StateBusinessCompliance](https://www.statebusinesscompliance.com/blog/delaware-llc-annual-tax-2026); [Wolters Kluwer](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/sv-se/expert-insights/delaware-annual-tax-is-due-june-1)

**83(b) election (IRS Form 15620, online filing)**
- IRS released **Form 15620** (Section 83(b) Election) in November 2024 and added **online submission in 2025**. Filers sign in with ID.me, answer guided questions, then either submit electronically or download and mail. — [Morrison Foerster (Jul 24, 2025)](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/250724-say-goodbye-to-paper-cuts-section-83-b); [Mintz (Jul 29, 2025)](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2906/2025-07-29-new-electronic-filing-option-section-83b-elections)
- Online filing gives proof of receipt. That matters because the **30-day deadline is strict** and late elections are not allowed. Filers should use one method only, not both. — [Morrison Foerster](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/250724-say-goodbye-to-paper-cuts-section-83-b); [Snell & Wilmer](https://www.swlaw.com/blogs/employee-benefits/2025/08/13/electronic-filing-now-available-for-section-83b-elections/)
- The rule to give a copy to the employer or issuer still applies. Some practitioners describe the online system as having "quirks" for founders. — [Daily Journal](https://dailyjournal.com/article/388813-83-b-goes-digital-quirks-founders-should-know-before-clicking-submit); [Shulman Rogers](https://www.shulmanrogers.com/legal-alert-online-filing-now-available-for-section-83b-elections/)
- Stripe Atlas organizes founder docs by task: 83(b) elections, incorporation documents, founder equity, bank accounts, business taxes, SAFE fundraising. It also says it doesn't provide legal, tax or accounting advice. — [Stripe Docs, Atlas](https://docs.stripe.com/atlas)

**BOI / Corporate Transparency Act: changed recently**
- FinCEN's March 2025 interim final rule exempted domestic companies. On **Aug 11, 2026** FinCEN announced a **final rule** (published in the Federal Register and effective **Aug 14, 2026**) that **permanently exempts domestic reporting companies and US persons** from BOI reporting. Only foreign-formed entities registered to do business in a US state or Tribal jurisdiction remain "reporting companies." — [Morrison Foerster (Aug 20, 2026)](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/260820-fincen-ends-beneficial-ownership-reporting); [Mayer Brown (Aug 2026)](https://www.mayerbrown.com/es/insights/publications/2026/08/the-final-chapter-fincen-permanently-eliminates-boi-reporting-requirements-for-us-companies-and-us-persons); [BDO](https://www.bdo.com/insights/tax/fincen-final-rule-permanently-relieves-us-companies-of-beneficial-ownership-reporting-obligations); [Troutman](https://www.troutman.com/insights/relief-is-real-but-not-universal-fincen-permanently-ends-boi-reporting-for-us-companies/)
- FinCEN will delete US-person BOI data already collected without being asked. Sources report it will not delete US-person data submitted after Feb 10, 2027. — [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/es/insights/publications/2026/08/the-final-chapter-fincen-permanently-eliminates-boi-reporting-requirements-for-us-companies-and-us-persons); [ABA Washington Letter](https://www.americanbar.org/advocacy/governmental_legislative_work/publications/washingtonletter/august-26-wl/cta-beneficial-ownership-reporting-0826wl/)
- Sources disagree on the date of the interim rule: some say effective March 25, 2025, others say issued March 26, 2025. — [Steptoe](https://www.steptoe.com/en/news-publications/international-compliance-blog/fincen-issues-final-rule-eliminating-beneficial-ownership-reporting-for-us-companies-and-us-persons.html); [McDermott](https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/fincen-narrows-beneficial-ownership-information-reporting-requirements/)
- Practitioners advise US companies to record their exemption status. — [Troutman](https://www.troutman.com/insights/relief-is-real-but-not-universal-fincen-permanently-ends-boi-reporting-for-us-companies/)

**Form D (SEC)**
- Form D is due **within 15 days after the first sale**. The first-sale date is when the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed. A weekend or holiday deadline moves to the next business day. It is filed on **EDGAR** with no SEC fee. — [SEC, Filing a Form D Notice](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/filing-form-d-notice); [SEC Corp Fin](https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/form-d-filing)
- Under the SEC's Rule 503 guidance, filing on time is **not a condition** of the 504/506(b)/506(c) exemptions. Late filers should still file "as soon as practicable." — [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/filing-form-d-notice)
- **EDGAR Next:** accounts granted through Form ID after March 24, 2025 already comply. Older filers had to enroll, which can trip up first-time startup filers. — [SEC Form D notice page](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/filing-form-d-notice)
- The SEC publishes plain-language "Capital Raising Building Blocks" pages for small businesses, including "What is Form D?". These are linkable federal content. — [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/form-d)
- A current Form D PDF exists with a Feb 18, 2026 date in its filename, so the form has been recently revised. — [SEC Form D PDF](https://www.sec.gov/files/form-d-02182026.pdf)

**EIN**
- An EIN is needed for federal taxes, hiring, bank accounts and business licenses or permits. — [SBA, Launch your business](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/apply-licenses-permits)

### Inferences
- The Startup checklist could have these items: certificate of incorporation, EIN (SS-4), 83(b) per founder (30-day timer starting at the grant date, with "proof of IRS receipt" stored as a passport item), Form D (15 days after first SAFE or priced-round close, plus state blue-sky notices), Delaware March 1 franchise tax and annual report, foreign qualification annual reports in the home state, and 409A refresh before option grants.
- The franchise-tax item should explain the Authorized Shares versus Assumed Par Value Capital Method. Startups with 10M or more authorized shares commonly get alarming bills under the default method. This is a high-value, low-risk explainer that can link to corp.delaware.gov.
- BOI content is a trust test: any "BOI deadline" template or reminder must be removed or relabeled "foreign companies only." Leftover BOI warnings would hurt credibility.
- "Delaware LLC tax: $300 → $400 (from June 2027 bill)" is a good example of a date-stamped "what changed" note.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch YC's post-money SAFE page, so its current template versions (cap-only, discount-only, MFN, pro-rata side letter) are not verified here. Check ycombinator.com/documents.
- 409A: no source gathered. The usual context is that an independent appraisal provides a safe-harbor presumption, typically refreshed every 12 months or after a material event. Verify against Treas. Reg. §1.409A-1(b)(5)(iv)(B) or a firm alert.
- Foreign qualification (state "certificate of authority") rules, fees and annual reports vary by state and were not researched.
- I could not open IRS's own Form 15620 / 83(b) online-filing page directly. The details come from several consistent law-firm alerts.
- The exact SS-4 / online EIN hours and process were not re-verified.

## Freelancer mode: W-9, 1099 thresholds 2026, estimated taxes, SE tax, freelance protection laws, contracts

### Takeaway
2026 is a threshold-change year:
- 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC reporting (and backup withholding) moved from **$600 to $2,000** for payments after Dec 31, 2025, indexed to inflation from 2027. IRS instructions confirm this.
- 1099-K returned to **$20,000 / 200 transactions** under OBBBA. This comes from secondary sources and needs IRS confirmation.

Freelancer mode should pair a W-9 "send kit" with a quarterly estimated-tax calendar (Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15) and state freelance-contract checklists (NY $800, IL $500, CA $250, NYC since 2017).

### Cited Findings
- **1099-NEC/MISC threshold is $2,000** for tax years beginning after 2025, and the same threshold applies to backup withholding. It "may be" inflation-adjusted starting in 2027. — [IRS Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC (Rev. Dec 2026)](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec); [IRS Publication 1099 (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p1099)
- Proposed regulations (REG-113229-25) confirm $2,000 for payments made after Dec 31, 2025 and before Jan 1, 2027. The statutory threshold applies even before the regulations are finalized. — [Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-19](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-19_IRB)
- A 1099-NEC must still be filed for any payee from whom federal income tax was withheld, whatever the amount. The backup withholding rate is 24%. — [IRS i1099mec](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec); [IRS 1099-NEC FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/faqs/small-business-self-employed-other-business/form-1099-nec-independent-contractors/form-1099-nec-independent-contractors)
- OBBBA was signed July 4, 2025. It moved the 1099-NEC/MISC threshold from $600 to $2,000, the first change since 1954 according to some coverage. It also restored the **1099-K threshold to $20,000 and 200 transactions**, replacing the planned $2,500 (2025) and $600 (2026) phase-down. — [CPA Practice Advisor](https://cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/07/30/one-big-beautiful-bill-act-changes-1099-thresholds/165863/); [Patriot Software](https://www.patriotsoftware.com/blog/accounting/1099-reporting-threshold/); [OnPay](https://onpay.com/insights/1099-reporting-threshold-updates/)
- **2026 estimated tax (Form 1040-ES) due dates: April 15, June 15, Sept 15, 2026, and Jan 15, 2027.** The January payment can be skipped if the 2026 return is filed by Feb 1, 2027 with full payment. Estimated tax generally applies when $1,000 or more is expected to be owed after withholding and credits. — [IRS 2026 Form 1040-ES](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1040es.pdf); [IRS Estimated tax FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/faqs/estimated-tax)
- Self-employment tax is computed on 92.35% of net SE earnings. The 2026 Social Security wage base is listed as $184,500. — [IRS 2026 Form 1040-ES](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1040es.pdf)
- IRS publishes quarterly small-business tax calendars that can be linked or mirrored. — [IRS First quarter calendar](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/first-quarter-tax-calendar); [IRS Third quarter](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/third-quarter-tax-calendar)
- **New York State Freelance Isn't Free Act:** applies to contracts on or after **Aug 28, 2024**. The **$800** threshold can be met by one contract or by aggregated contracts over 120 days. A written contract must state the services, rate and method of pay, and payment date. The hiring party keeps it **6 years**. One firm says it excludes attorneys, licensed medical professionals and construction contractors. One source gives a conflicting May 20, 2024 effective date. — [Vedder Price](https://www.vedderprice.com/new-york-states-freelance-isnt-free-act-takes-effect-august-28-2024); [Colligan Law](https://insights.colliganlaw.com/post/102itsx/new-law-protects-new-york-independent-contractors)
- **NYC** Freelance Isn't Free law in effect since May 15, 2017. — [Bond Schoeneck & King](https://www.bsk.com/new-york-labor-and-employment-law-report/new-york-city-law-protecting-freelance-workers-goes-into-effect-on-may-15-2017/print)
- **Illinois Freelance Worker Protection Act:** signed Aug 2023, effective **July 1, 2024**, written-contract threshold **$500**. Anti-retaliation rules apply, plus double damages and attorneys' fees. — [Foster Garvey](https://www.foster.com/?p=3504); [Staffing Industry Analysts](https://www.staffingindustry.com/editorial/cws-30-contingent-workforce-strategies/new-york-becomes-latest-state-pass-ic-protection)
- **California SB 988 (Freelance Worker Protection Act):** effective **Jan 1, 2025**.
  - Covers work of **$250 or more** (over 120 days / 4 months by some readings).
  - The hiring party must provide a signed written contract and keep it **4 years**.
  - Required terms: names and mailing addresses, itemized services, value and rate/method of pay, payment date or mechanism, and the deadline for submitting the list of services.
  - Payment is due within **30 days of completion** if no date is set.
  - Enforced by the Labor Commissioner, the AG and private action, with double damages for non-payment.
  - — [Sen. Wiener press release](https://sd11.senate.ca.gov/news/governor-newsom-signs-senator-wieners-freelance-worker-bill-law); [4A's](https://www.aaaa.org/blog/new-california-freelancer-law-takes-effect-on-january-1-2025/); [Buchalter PDF](https://buchalter.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/The-Freelance-Worker-Protection-Act.KFox_.Lopez2_.pdf)

### Inferences
- Freelancer onboarding should ask for state and city (NYC, NY, IL, CA, plus others such as LA or Seattle, unverified) and switch on a "contract must include" checklist for the relevant law. This is a factual checklist, not advice.
- 1099 content must be tax-year-specific. "$600" pages for 2026 payments are now wrong. A threshold table could read: tax year 2025 = $600 (NEC); 2026 = $2,000; 2027+ = indexed.
- A W-9 module: store the TIN securely once, generate a signed W-9 on request, and log who received it and when (this feeds the paperwork passport).

### Gaps
- No IRS page confirming the 1099-K $20,000/200 revert was retrieved. Confirm on irs.gov/1099k.
- The self-employment tax rate (15.3% = 12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare, plus 0.9% Additional Medicare above thresholds) was not captured from a primary source here. Verify on the IRS self-employment tax page.
- The current W-9 revision date (Rev. March 2024 is believed current) was not verified.
- Freelance laws in other cities (LA, Seattle, Columbus, Minneapolis?) were not researched. The NY and IL details come from 2023–2024 law-firm alerts, and no later amendments were checked.

## Small business mode (restaurants/retail): licenses, seller's permits, DBA, food permits, signage, ADA, sales tax, annual reports

### Takeaway
SBA's own guidance defers almost every specific to state and local agencies. Small Business mode's value is therefore a jurisdiction-aware checklist with linked official sources: DBA (state, county or city), state entity registration and annual report, state sales-tax permit, local business license, health permits, sign permits, and renewals.

### Cited Findings
- Businesses may need to register a DBA (trade, fictitious or assumed name) with the state, county or city. A DBA alone gives no legal protection. Most states require registration if a DBA is used, a few don't, and some handle it through the county clerk. — [SBA, Register your business](https://legacy.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business)
- Local governments set their own registration, licensing and permit requirements. Federal licenses apply only to regulated activities, such as alcohol, firearms and aviation. — [SBA, Apply for licenses and permits](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/apply-licenses-permits)
- Sellers of regulated items (tobacco, alcohol, tires) may need to renew sales permits regularly. The state revenue department issues seller's permits. — [SBA, Register With State Agencies](https://www.sba.gov/node/2796)
- SBA district office pages ("Doing business in the Sacramento District," "San Diego District") localize these pointers. — [SBA Sacramento](https://www.sba.gov/district/sacramento/doing-business-sacramento-district)

### Inferences
- Restaurant and retail checklist template (to be built from state sources): seller's permit or sales-tax registration; filing frequency (monthly, quarterly or annual, set by the state from volume); local business license and its renewal; food-service permit from the county health department; food handler or manager certification; alcohol license (state ABC plus local); sign permit; certificate of occupancy; fire inspection; state annual or biennial report. Every row needs a "verify with [agency]" link.
- ADA basics should link to ADA.gov guides only, and never say "you are compliant."

### Gaps
- No primary-source survey of state annual-report deadlines or sales-tax filing schedules was done. A 50-state table needs separate research from each Secretary of State and Department of Revenue.
- ADA.gov small-business resources, FDA Food Code adoption by state, and sign-permit norms were not researched.
- State "one-stop" business portals (e.g., CA CalGold, NY Business Express, TX Governor's Office of Small Business Assistance) were not verified in this pass.

## Contractor/Insurance mode: COIs (ACORD 25 licensing), AI endorsements, lien waivers, license lookups, workers' comp, W-9s, prequalification

### Takeaway
Contractor mode revolves around four things: COIs on ACORD 25, endorsements (CG 20 10/20 37 additional insured, CG 20 01 primary and noncontributory, CG 24 04 / WC 00 03 13 waivers of subrogation), statutory lien-waiver forms in about 11–12 states, and W-9s. **ACORD forms are copyrighted and licensed, and the COI should be issued by the insurance agent.** ShipShape should **store, track and request** COIs and help users understand them. It should not generate or fill ACORD 25s. A new **ACORD 25 (2025/12) edition** replaced 2016/03, and the 12-month license window ends around **Dec 1, 2026**.

### Cited Findings
- ACORD's license allows a replaced edition to be used for **no more than 12 months** after the new edition is published. Big I NY says issuing ACORD 25 (2016/03) after **Dec 1, 2026** would violate the ACORD license. That is a license violation, not a legal one. — [Big I New York, Apr 3, 2026](https://www.biginy.org/news/ask-tim-news/the-old-acord-certificate-form-is-not-illegal-stop-using-it/)
- ACORD announced **ACORD 25 (2025/12)** in Dec 2025. NY DFS removed 2016/03 from its approved certificate list. The new edition changes the wording on limits. — [Big I NY](https://www.biginy.org/news/ask-tim-news/the-old-acord-certificate-form-is-not-illegal-stop-using-it/); [Big I NY related post](https://www.biginy.org/?p=11117)
- ACORD's FAQ (as quoted) says it no longer checks outdated forms for compliance with law or state insurance department rules. — [Big I NY](https://www.biginy.org/news/ask-tim-news/the-old-acord-certificate-form-is-not-illegal-stop-using-it/)
- The ACORD 25 is described as a copyrighted document. — [IIABA one-pager](https://na.iiaba.net/vu/SiteAssets/Pages/featured-resources/certificates-public/certificates-cancellation/ACORD25CancellationNoticeOnePager.doc)
- NY DFS Opinion (2002): certificates only **evidence** insurance and cannot amend, expand or alter the policy. NY Insurance Law Article 5 (§502) limits what certificate requesters can demand. — [NY DFS OGC Opinion 02-12-04](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/insurance/ogco2002/rg021204.htm); [Big I NY](https://www.biginy.org/news/ask-tim-news/the-old-acord-certificate-form-is-not-illegal-stop-using-it/)
- **Endorsements**
  - CG 20 10 covers ongoing operations; CG 20 37 covers completed operations.
  - Owners often name specific editions, e.g., "CG 20 10 (07-04) and CG 20 37 (07-04)" or "CG 20 10 11 85."
  - CG 20 01 = primary and noncontributory; CG 24 04 = GL waiver of subrogation; WC 00 03 13 = workers' comp waiver of subrogation.
  - Commentators warn ISO AI forms are not always favorable to additional insureds in construction.
  - — [Jones, CG 20 10 12 19](https://getjones.com/endorsements/general-liability/CG20101219); [Jones, CG 20 37 12 19](https://getjones.com/endorsements/general-liability/CG20371219); [Iowa State sample COI requirements](https://www.fpm.iastate.edu/project_management/forms/Payment%20and%20Performance/Contractor/Sample%20Certificate%20of%20Insurance%202013.pdf); [Sierra Madre sample insurance forms (Oct 2025)](https://www.sierramadreca.gov/media/ibbczcd1/sierra-madra-sample-insurance-forms-10242025.pdf); [Construction Executive](https://constructionexec.com/?p=10761); [NetVendor endorsements FAQ](https://faq.netvendor.com/article/219-common-industry-standard-endorsements)
- **Statutory lien waivers:** about 12 states prescribe forms.
  - CA (Civ. Code §§8124, 8132–8138), AZ (A.R.S. §33-1008; nonconforming waivers unenforceable), TX (Prop. Code §§53.281–.284) and NV all use conditional and unconditional, progress and final forms.
  - Georgia regulates form details down to font size.
  - Florida (Fla. Stat. §713.20) offers forms that "may" be used, which is why some lists count 11 states.
  - Others: MA, MI, MS, MO (residential only), UT, WY.
  - — [Bradley, "Wait, Is My Lien Waiver Enforceable?" (Oct 2023)](https://www.bradley.com/insights/publications/2023/10/wait-is-my-lien-waiver-enforceable); [Levelset free lien waiver forms](https://www.levelset.com/lien-waivers/free-lien-waiver-forms/); [ezel.ai state survey](https://ezel.ai/surveys/mechanics-lien-waiver-rules)
- AIA released sworn construction statement and lien waiver/release forms (G706-type) for the first time in 2022. These are another copyrighted, licensed form family. — [NASBP / Robinson+Cole](https://www.nasbp.org/post/the-aia-releases-sworn-construction-statements-lien-waiver-and-release-forms-for-the-first-time/)
- Texas Department of Insurance hosts certificate documents, so state regulators are a source for approved certificate forms. — [TDI](https://www.Tdi.texas.gov/certificates/documentcr/119313.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Product rule:** COIs are uploaded or requested from the agent. ShipShape extracts the fields (insured, carriers, policy numbers, limits, dates, AI/WOS checkboxes, certificate holder), tracks expiration dates, and auto-drafts a "please send an updated COI with these endorsements" email to the agent. Don't generate ACORD forms. A COI can't add coverage (NY DFS), so a "COI Explainer" must say that the endorsement, not the certificate, controls.
- The edition change gives a time-limited content angle: "ACORD 25 2016/03 vs 2025/12: what changed and why you might get a new certificate after Dec 1, 2026." Keep it factual and cite Big I NY.
- Lien waivers: for statutory states, link to the statute text, offer the exact statutory form (where the state's text is public law) as a fill-in, and warn "statutory form must be used verbatim in [state]." For non-statutory states, use a generic template with a strong "have counsel review" warning.
- Prequalification packets usually contain W-9, COI, license number and expiry, workers' comp certificate or exemption, EMR/safety data and references. This is ideal content for the paperwork passport.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve ACORD's actual forms license or terms of use from acord.org. Whether non-agent software may reproduce, render or fill ACORD 25 is **not verified**. Get the license terms or legal review before any ACORD-rendering feature. ACORD distributes forms to members and licensed vendors, but this is not documented here.
- Workers' comp exemption certificates (e.g., FL DFS Construction Exemption, CA/TX rules) were not researched and vary by state.
- Contractor license lookup portals (CSLB in CA, TDLR in TX, FL DBPR, etc.) were not catalogued here.
- ISO endorsement forms are also copyrighted (Verisk/ISO). Reproduction rights were not researched.

## Authoritative public resource hubs and reuse licensing (SBA, IRS, SCORE, SBDC, state portals)

### Takeaway
Content written by the federal government (IRS, SBA, SEC, FinCEN) is not subject to US copyright under 17 U.S.C. §105. ShipShape can quote and adapt it with attribution, but must not imply endorsement and must check for embedded third-party copyrighted material. State-government content is **not** automatically public domain.

### Cited Findings
- 17 U.S.C. §105: "Copyright protection under this title is not available for any work of the United States Government." — [Justia, 17 U.S.C. §105](https://law.justia.com/codes/us/2010/title17/chap1/sec105/)
- SBA's reproduction and linking policy says government information on SBA.gov is in the public domain and may be freely distributed and copied, and asks that SBA be credited. ### Inferences
- Linking strategy: deep-link the official form page (irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-XXXX, sec.gov, corp.delaware.gov, state SOS/DOR) and show "Source: IRS, retrieved YYYY-MM-DD." Mirror official PDFs only with integrity checks, and prefer linking so users get the current revision.

## Competitor resource libraries, SEO value of "how to fill out Form X," and advice-risk mitigation

### Takeaway
Reliable data was sparse. The verified example is Stripe Atlas: task-organized guides written by entrepreneurs and experts, some vetted by outside lawyers and accountants, free templates, and an explicit "no legal/tax advice" stance. Google treats legal and finance content as YMYL, where E-E-A-T and accuracy matter most. The structure of LegalZoom, Gusto, Bench, NerdWallet, Clerky, Carta and Mercury libraries was mostly **not verified** in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Stripe Atlas guides say they are written by experienced entrepreneurs and experts, with some vetted by outside lawyers and accountants. They are meant to be understood by first-time founders but still useful to experienced ones. Templates were built with input from accounting, tax and legal experts and are free to download. — [Stripe Atlas guides (archived)](https://web-archive.nli.org.il/National_Library/mp_/https://stripe.com/atlas/guides); [VentureBeat on Atlas launch](https://venturebeat.com/business/stripe-opens-atlas-business-creation-toolkit-to-u-s-entrepreneurs)
- Stripe Atlas docs organize content by founder task (83(b), incorporation documents, equity, banking, taxes, SAFE) and state that Atlas doesn't provide legal, tax or accounting advice. — [Stripe Docs, Atlas](https://docs.stripe.com/atlas)
- NerdWallet small-business content shows several trust signals:
  - specialist writers and editors;
  - "editorial integrity guidelines";
  - human fact-checking with "accurate as of publication" language;
  - credibility stats (250+ products reviewed);
  - press mentions;
  - an advertiser disclosure, because partners pay for placement.
  - — [NerdWallet Gusto review](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/reviews/gusto)
- Google's July 2022 rater-guidelines update redefined YMYL around potential for harm and assessed it on a spectrum. For highly YMYL pages, E-E-A-T matters above other factors, and accuracy is crucial. Raters do not directly set rankings. — [Search Engine Land (Jul 2022)](https://searchengineland.com/google-search-quality-rater-guidelines-changes-july-2022-386815); [Wix SEO Hub](https://www.wix.com/seo/learn/resource/google-search-quality-guidelines); [Thrive Agency](https://thriveagency.com/news/google-search-quality-rater-guidelines-update-everything-you-need-to-know)
- Vendors already publish SEO-oriented form guides, for example "ACORD 25 Form: Download PDF & Step-by-Step Guide" and "Free Lien Waiver Forms" (Levelset), so the "how to fill out X" keyword space is contested. — [Eduyush ACORD 25 guide](https://eduyush.com/blogs/insurance/acord-25-form); [Levelset](https://www.levelset.com/lien-waivers/free-lien-waiver-forms/)
- Payroll and accounting vendors (Patriot, OnPay, Xero) published 1099-threshold explainers within weeks of OBBBA. That pattern of timely "what changed" content earns links. — [Patriot Software](https://www.patriotsoftware.com/blog/accounting/1099-reporting-threshold/); [OnPay](https://onpay.com/insights/1099-reporting-threshold-updates/); [Xero](https://www.xero.com/us/accountant-bookkeeper-guides/1099-filing-requirements/)

### Inferences
- Recommended page template for each form or deadline:
  1. what it is (one line);
  2. who usually files it (general);
  3. official source link and form revision date;
  4. deadline or recurrence;
  5. fields explained, quoting IRS instructions where possible;
  6. "what changed" box with dates;
  7. "When to ask a professional";
  8. "Last reviewed YYYY-MM-DD by [named reviewer, credential]";
  9. disclaimer.

  This mirrors the Stripe Atlas and NerdWallet signals.
- SEO: "how to fill out Form W-9 / 1099-NEC / 15620 / ACORD 25 / CA conditional waiver" pages work best when the page links straight into the in-app fill flow. Their main advantage is freshness (year-specific titles like "1099-NEC threshold 2026: $2,000").
- Risk control: keep language descriptive ("the IRS instructions say…"), never prescriptive ("you should elect…"). Version every page, and auto-flag pages tied to forms whose revision date changes.

### Gaps
- No verified data on how LegalZoom, Gusto, Bench, Clerky, Carta or Mercury structure their resource centers (taxonomy, reviewer bylines, update cadence). Searches returned nothing usable. Direct fetches of each help center or blog are needed.
- No primary Google Search Central source was fetched on "helpful content" or visible update dates. No traffic or ranking data was found for "how to fill out Form X" queries.
- No case law or regulatory source was gathered on unauthorized-practice-of-law (UPL) risk for document-drafting software (e.g., LegalZoom UPL cases, state bar opinions). This is a significant gap for the "drafts simple documents with warnings" feature.
