# Online Legal-Document & Business-Formation Competitors to ShipShape (as of Oct 2026, US focus)

Research scope: Rocket Lawyer, LegalZoom, Clerky, Stripe Atlas, Common Paper, Bonterms, Docracy, US Legal Forms, Bizee/Incfile, ZenBusiness, Northwest Registered Agent, and 2025-2026 AI legal-doc entrants. Research was limited to about 17 search/fetch calls. Many pricing figures come from affiliate comparison sites; I mark them as such. Wherever possible I verified against official pages: the Rocket Lawyer pricing page, the LegalZoom press release and annual report, the FTC, and Bonterms.

## Q1. How do they monetize (membership, per-document, attorney upsells, registered-agent renewals)?

### Takeaway
Most incumbents run a recurring-revenue business. LegalZoom gets about 65% of its revenue from subscriptions: registered agent, compliance, attorney plans, eSignature, virtual mail and so on. Rocket Lawyer sells all-you-can-eat memberships and adds paid attorney sessions and filing add-ons on top. Formation-first players (ZenBusiness, Bizee, Northwest) win customers with cheap or free formation, then earn on registered-agent renewals. The free standards bodies (Common Paper, Bonterms) give away CC BY templates; Common Paper charges for its contract-workflow software.

The company attributes the ARPU gain mainly to the Formation Nation acquisition, partly offset by a mix shift to lower-priced offerings. Results were reported February 2026. — [LegalZoom FY2025 press release](https://investors.legalzoom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/legalzoom-reports-strong-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025); [LegalZoom FY2025 ARS (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001286139/000119312526170358/d72934dars.pdf)

Transaction units fell 1% to about 239k, which the company tied to the elimination of BOIR (beneficial ownership report) filings. — [LegalZoom FY2025 press release](https://investors.legalzoom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/legalzoom-reports-strong-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025)
- About 65% of 2025 revenue came from subscriptions. The 10-K groups subscription products into three buckets. Compliance covers registered agent, compliance subscriptions and a "DIFM" (do-it-for-me) compliance concierge. Legal covers small-business and consumer attorney-advice plans and legal forms. - Registered-agent pricing ranges from $99 to $249/yr for essentially the same statutory service. That spread suggests customers are not price-sensitive once locked in, because switching agents requires a state filing.

## Q2. What lawsuits and regulatory actions have they faced, and why?

### Takeaway
LegalZoom has the deepest litigation history. Its unauthorized-practice-of-law (UPL) fights were largely resolved by settlements and statutes between 2011 and 2016. A 2024 New Jersey UPL class action was pushed into individual arbitration in 2025. The most important recent regulatory precedent for AI legal products is the FTC's 2025 DoNotPay "robot lawyer" order. I found no court-confirmed class action specifically over registered-agent auto-renewal; consumer complaints about renewal and cancellation billing are widespread.

### Cited Findings
**LegalZoom: UPL history**
- Janson v. LegalZoom (W.D. Mo., 2011): the court held that LegalZoom's document preparation, other than patents and trademarks (which are federally preempted), was unauthorized practice of law. The court located the problem in the role of human employees, not the internet medium. The case settled before a final merits ruling, which mooted the summary-judgment order. — [Studicata case brief](https://www.studicata.com/case-briefs/case/janson-v-legalzoom-com-inc); [ElderLawAnswers on class certification](https://attorney.elderlawanswers.com/missouri-court-certifies-unauthorized-practice-of-law-class-action-against-legalzoom-8830)
- North Carolina: the State Bar sent cease-and-desist letters and LegalZoom sued the bar. The settlement let such companies operate if they register with the state and follow consumer-protection procedures. In 2016 the legislature defined the practice of law to exclude software that generates documents from a user's answers. — [ABA Journal](https://www.abajournal.com/magazine/article/latest_legal_victory_has_legalzoom_poised_for_growth); [Wikipedia: LegalZoom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LegalZoom)
- In March 2014 the South Carolina Supreme Court held that selling documents produced from interactive questionnaires is not UPL. In 2013 the Arkansas Supreme Court enforced LegalZoom's arbitration clause against UPL claims. — [Wikipedia: LegalZoom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LegalZoom)
- A separate UPL suit alleged that non-lawyers at LegalZoom did legal work on trademark applications. — [ABA Journal](https://www.abajournal.com/news/article/LegalZoom_UPL_lawsuit_trademark) (date not captured)
- Erasmus v. LegalZoom (D.N.J.):
  - June 2024: a New Jersey lawyer filed a proposed UPL class action. It argued that LegalZoom's structure, including non-lawyer owners, does not fit New Jersey's professional-service-corporation rules. — [Above the Law, June 2024](https://abovethelaw.com/legal-innovation-center/2024/06/06/legalzoom-sued-for-unauthorized-practice-of-law-in-state-that-still-recognizes-alina-habba/); [Complaint (classaction.org)](https://www.classaction.org/media/erasmus-v-legalzoom-com-inc-et-al.pdf)
  - On April 17, 2025, the federal judge compelled arbitration, based on the plaintiff clicking "agree and pay now". — [Law360](https://law360.com/ip/articles/2326527&read_later=1&nlsidx=1&nlaidx=6); [MLex](https://www.mlex.com/pulse/articles/1867594)
- A 2017 New Jersey joint ethics opinion said NJ lawyers may not participate in LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer or Avvo legal-service plans, because those plans are not registered with the Administrative Office of the Courts. — [NJACPE/ezel.ai summary, June 2017](https://ezel.ai/ethics-opinions/njacpe/732-avvo-legalzoom-rocket-lawyer-legal-service-plans)
- The FY2025 10-K concedes that its model "has subjected us to allegations of UPL" and that it "cannot assure you that it will insulate us from claims of CPL or UPL." It also calls its Arizona Alternative Business Structure (ABS) license, granted September 2021, "generally untested in U.S. courts." — [StockTitan 10-K summary](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/LZ/10-k-legalzoom-com-inc-files-annual-report-d32f4eba4f4f.html)
- Securities-class-action investigations were announced by plaintiff firms in 2023 and 2024. These are investor matters, not consumer ones. — [Business Wire, Feb 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230223005899/en/LEGALZOOM-ALERT-Bragar-Eagel-Squire-P.C.-is-Investigating-LegalZoom-Inc.-on-Behalf-of-LegalZoom-Stockholders-and-Encourages-Investors-to-Contact-the-Firm); [Business Wire, July 2024](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240731534135/en/Rosen-Law-Firm-Encourages-LegalZoom.com-Inc.-Investors-to-Inquire-About-Securities-Class-Action-Investigation-%E2%80%93-LZ)
- A class action filed around 2010 alleged unfair and misleading business practices; I did not find its outcome. — [Top Class Actions](https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/lawsuit-news/legalzoom-faces-class-action-lawsuit-over-deceptive-business-practices/)
- LegalZoom's Terms of Service require individual dispute resolution: no class arbitration, class action or representative proceeding. — [LegalZoom Terms of Service](https://www.legalzoom.com/legal/general-terms/terms-of-service)

**Auto-renewal**
- I found no class action specifically targeting LegalZoom's registered-agent auto-renewal. There are individual consumer complaints, such as a $399 renewal charged after the customer said they had cancelled. — [Ripoff Report](https://www.ripoffreport.com/report/legalzoom/twice-charged-renewal-fees-i-1481293) (unverified consumer post)

**FTC v. DoNotPay (AI "robot lawyer")**
- The FTC complaint was announced in September 2024. It said DoNotPay marketed itself as "the world's first robot lawyer" but never tested whether its AI performed at the level of a human lawyer and never hired attorneys to test its law-related features. — [FTC press release, Feb 2025](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/02/ftc-finalizes-order-donotpay-prohibits-deceptive-ai-lawyer-claims-imposes-monetary-relief-requires)
- The final order was approved 5-0 on January 16, 2025 and finalized in February 2025. It requires $193,000 in monetary relief, notice to 2021-2023 subscribers, and a ban on claims of lawyer-equivalent performance without substantiation. — [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/02/ftc-finalizes-order-donotpay-prohibits-deceptive-ai-lawyer-claims-imposes-monetary-relief-requires); [FTC case timeline](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/donotpay-timeline-item-2025-02-11)

**Rocket Lawyer**
- Complaints center on billing and cancellation, not document quality:
  - continued charges after cancellation
  - free trials converting to paid without clear disclosure
  - unresponsive support for refunds
  — [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/legal/rocket-lawyer.html); [PissedConsumer](https://rocket-lawyer.pissedconsumer.com/review.html)
- Ratings diverge by platform: about 1.6/5 on PissedConsumer (59 reviews), versus 4.2-4.7/5 and a reported BBB A+ on an aggregator. — [PissedConsumer](https://rocket-lawyer.pissedconsumer.com/review.html); [checkthat.ai reviews](https://checkthat.ai/brands/rocket-lawyer/reviews)

### Inferences
- UPL risk now attaches mainly to human involvement (non-lawyer staff reviewing or customizing documents) and to "lawyer-like" marketing claims (DoNotPay), not to software that fills blanks from user answers. That is ShipShape's planned lane.
- Arbitration clauses with class waivers are the incumbents' main litigation shield (Arkansas 2013, New Jersey 2025). Expect them to remain standard.
- The FTC DoNotPay order sets a clear line: do not call AI features a "lawyer" or imply lawyer-equivalent quality without testing.

### Gaps
- I found no FTC or state AG action against LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer, ZenBusiness, Bizee or Northwest specifically over auto-renewal. I also did not check the FTC "click-to-cancel" rule status or state ARL (auto-renewal law) enforcement in 2025-2026.
- I could not find the outcome of the Erasmus arbitration or of any appeal.
- No litigation research was done on Clerky, Stripe Atlas, Common Paper or Bonterms; none surfaced in searches.
- I did not get Trustpilot or BBB complaint counts directly for LegalZoom, ZenBusiness or Bizee.

## Q3. How do they structure disclaimers and attorney referral to stay legal?

### Takeaway
The standard pattern has four parts:
1. A "we are not a law firm / not a lawyer referral service" disclaimer.
2. Self-help software that fills documents from user answers.
3. Legal advice only from independent licensed attorneys in a network, sold as metered sessions or plans.
4. Mandatory individual arbitration.

LegalZoom adds an Arizona ABS license. AI features are framed as "efficiency tools", with escalation to human attorneys.

### Cited Findings
- Rocket Lawyer calls itself a "legal technology company". It says it is not a lawyer referral service, accounting firm or law firm, and that it "does not provide legal representation." Legal advice is given only in Legal Advice Sessions by "independent, licensed attorneys" in its network. Its AI is "an efficiency tool, not a substitute for attorney credentials." — [Rocket Lawyer pricing page](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/pricing)
- LegalZoom holds an Arizona ABS license (September 2021), which lets non-lawyer-owned entities deliver legal services in Arizona. The 10-K calls the license untested in courts. — [StockTitan 10-K summary](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/LZ/10-k-legalzoom-com-inc-files-annual-report-d32f4eba4f4f.html)
- On February 24, 2026, LegalZoom launched a "Connector" inside Anthropic's Claude. When Claude flags a high-risk clause or compliance issue, the user can book a LegalZoom network attorney, and Claude's analysis is passed along. The attorneys are independent, not LegalZoom employees. It is marketed as "no hourly billing, no retainers", with no prices disclosed. — [LegalZoom press release](https://www.legalzoom.com/press-releases/legalzoom-partners-with-claude-powered-by-anthropic); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260224076109/en/legalzoom-disrupts-the-billable-hour-by-bringing-attorney-accountability-to-claude-powered-by-anthropic/)
- North Carolina's 2016 statute protects software that generates documents from user answers, provided the provider registers and follows consumer-protection conditions. — [ABA Journal](https://www.abajournal.com/magazine/article/latest_legal_victory_has_legalzoom_poised_for_growth); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LegalZoom)
- Bonterms' FAQ advises that legal counsel review a Standard Agreement before use. — [Bonterms FAQ](https://support.bonterms.com/hc/en-us/articles/17912468621972-Standard-Agreement-FAQs)
- New Jersey's 2017 ethics opinion barred NJ lawyers from participating in unregistered online legal-service plans. This shows that attorney-network models face state-by-state ethics constraints. — [NJACPE summary](https://ezel.ai/ethics-opinions/njacpe/732-avvo-legalzoom-rocket-lawyer-legal-service-plans)

### Inferences
- ShipShape's "no legal advice, user picks the form, user accepts yes/no" design matches the safest posture under the precedents above: the North Carolina 2016 statute, South Carolina 2014, and Janson's focus on human employees. To preserve that posture:
  - avoid human staff customizing documents
  - avoid "AI lawyer" language
  - consider registering in North Carolina if it offers templates
  - add clear "not a law firm" plus "consult an attorney" language
- If ShipShape ever adds attorney referral, a paid referral model may trigger state lawyer-referral-service rules. Rocket Lawyer explicitly disclaims being a referral service, which hints at that sensitivity.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the full LegalZoom or Rocket Lawyer Terms and privacy policies. I therefore have no verified statements on whether either uses customer documents or data to train AI models, or on their data-retention and sharing practices. This is a key gap for ShipShape's privacy positioning.
- I did not verify Stripe Atlas or Clerky disclaimer language.

## Q4. Which templates are freely licensed (Common Paper, Bonterms CC BY) and which are proprietary?

### Takeaway
Common Paper and Bonterms publish B2B standard agreements under CC BY 4.0, free to use and modify with attribution. Bonterms also releases its playbook items under CC0. These are mostly SaaS and commercial contracts, not small-business operational forms. LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer and US Legal Forms templates are proprietary and gated behind membership.

### Cited Findings
- Common Paper:
  - All its agreements are released under CC BY 4.0, usable "in any way, as long as you leave in the attribution." — [Common Paper Standards](https://commonpaper.com/standards/)
  - The library includes Cloud Service Agreement (SaaS), Professional Services Agreement, Partnership Agreement, Software License Agreement, among others. — [Common Paper Standards](https://commonpaper.com/standards/); [PSA](https://commonpaper.com/standards/professional-services-agreement/); [Cloud Service Agreement](https://commonpaper.com/standards/cloud-service-agreement/)
  - Each agreement has a deal-specific Cover Page plus fixed Standard Terms, so parties review once and reuse. — [Common Paper Standards](https://commonpaper.com/standards/)
  - The agreements are drafted by a committee of attorneys from vendors, procurement, boutique firms and Big Law. — [Common Paper Standards](https://commonpaper.com/standards/); [Artificial Lawyer, Dec 2022](https://www.artificiallawyer.com/2022/12/06/common-paper-launches-saas-agreement-toolkit-to-help-drive-standardisation/)
- Bonterms:
  - Its Standard Agreements are free and released under CC BY 4.0 "unless otherwise noted". Its Standard Playbook Items are released under CC0 1.0. — [Bonterms Standard Agreement FAQs](https://support.bonterms.com/hc/en-us/articles/17912468621972-Standard-Agreement-FAQs)
  - The committee has 120+ in-house lawyers, partners and subject-matter experts and was established in 2021. A 2023 article cited about 60 members, now outdated. — [Bonterms Committee](https://bonterms.com/committee); [Artificial Lawyer, Jan 2023](https://www.artificiallawyer.com/2023/01/05/whats-a-standard-the-bonterms-perspective/)
  - Forms include Cloud Terms, a Business Associate Agreement (BAA), an End User Agreement and a PSA. — [Bonterms Cloud Terms](https://bonterms.com/forms/bonterms-cloud-terms-v1); [BAA](https://bonterms.com/forms/baa-signable-version); [Download Center](https://bonterms.com/download-center/)
- Rocket Lawyer documents are available as "unlimited documents" only within a paid membership. — [Rocket Lawyer pricing](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/pricing)
- LegalZoom lists "legal forms" as part of its subscription "Legal" bucket. — [StockTitan 10-K summary](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/LZ/10-k-legalzoom-com-inc-files-annual-report-d32f4eba4f4f.html)

### Inferences
- ShipShape could legally bundle, autofill and attribute Common Paper and Bonterms agreements: mutual NDA, PSA, cloud/SaaS terms and similar. This is a ready-made, attorney-vetted, freely licensed contract library, which avoids drafting its own templates (a UPL-adjacent risk). Attribution must be preserved. Check each form for "unless otherwise noted" exceptions.
- Government forms (state, IRS, local license PDFs) are generally public. Pointing to and autofilling official forms, plus CC BY standards, would give ShipShape a template supply without proprietary licensing.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm Docracy's status. It is believed to be defunct (a crowdsourced open-document site from the 2010s), but no source was found in this session.
- I did not verify whether Stripe Atlas or Clerky templates are licensed for reuse; they appear to be proprietary and in-product.
- I did not verify Y Combinator's SAFE license terms.
- I found no US Legal Forms information: pricing, license or form count.

## Q5. What gaps could a newcomer focused on remembering, filling, storing and sharing (not legal advice) exploit?

### Takeaway
Incumbents monetize compliance renewals and attorney upsells, and are moving toward AI plus "human-in-the-loop" legal services. That is the opposite direction from ShipShape. Recurring complaints are about billing, auto-renewal and cancellation friction. There is still white space in four areas:
- **Neutral, low-cost storage of a business profile:** autofill across arbitrary official PDFs.
- **Cross-party sharing:** a "passport" link and two-way document requests.
- **Transparent billing:** fixed, simple pricing that avoids renewal traps.
- **Clear AI positioning:** no "lawyer" claims.

### Cited Findings
- Incumbents' AI direction:
  - LegalZoom's March 2026 AI-Powered Business License Research is a multi-agent system that automates license research and fulfillment across "tens of thousands of jurisdictions". — [LegalZoom press release, Mar 2026](https://www.legalzoom.com/press-releases/legalzooms-ai-powered-business-license-platform)
  - LegalZoom also monitors licensing requirements across about 90,000 jurisdictions. — [LegalZoom compliance press release](https://investors.legalzoom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/legalzoom-launches-new-compliance-product-solving-complexity)
  - LegalZoom has a ChatGPT plugin for attorneys. — [LegalZoom ChatGPT plugin press release](https://www.legalzoom.com/press-releases/legalzoom-launches-chatgpt-plugin-to-help-attorneys-work-more-efficiently)
  - LegalZoom has the Claude Connector (February 2026). — [LegalZoom press release](https://www.legalzoom.com/press-releases/legalzoom-partners-with-claude-powered-by-anthropic)
  - Rocket Copilot offers unlimited AI Q&A and contract review. — [Rocket Lawyer pricing](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/pricing)
- LegalZoom also says it uses "strategic AI partnerships" to be visible in AI answer engines. This means AI assistants are becoming an acquisition channel for incumbents. — [StockTitan 10-K summary](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/LZ/10-k-legalzoom-com-inc-files-annual-report-d32f4eba4f4f.html)
- Customer pain points:
  - post-cancellation charges, trial-to-paid conversion and support friction (Rocket Lawyer) — [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/legal/rocket-lawyer.html); [PissedConsumer](https://rocket-lawyer.pissedconsumer.com/review.html)
  - registered-agent auto-renewal that is hard to cancel, because a replacement agent must be filed with the state (LegalZoom) — [LegalZoom RA page](https://www.legalzoom.com/business/business-operations/registered-agent-overview.html)
- The AI legal-tech money is going upmarket. In 2026, Harvey raised $200M at an $11B valuation and Legora raised $600M Series D at $5.6B. SMB-focused players are smaller: Spellbook (about $80M raised, Word-based drafting, small-firm focus) and Lawhive (£9.5M seed for "main street" law firms). — [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/top-legal-ai-startups/); [TechCrunch on Lawhive](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2691416); [Law.com on Newcode $13.5M Series A, Aug 2026](https://www.law.com/legaltechnews/2026/08/24/legal-ai-startup-newcode-announces-135m-series-a-round-with-investment-from-relativity/) (aggregator figures are unverified against primary releases)
- One tracker counted 33 disclosed legal-AI deals ($1.17B) from August 2025 to July 2026, mostly enterprise and law-firm focused. — [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/top-legal-ai-startups/)
- Startup-formation tools focus narrowly on Delaware C-corps and fundraising paperwork:
  - Stripe Atlas: one-time $500 — [Rho](https://www.rho.co/blog/stripe-atlas-review)
  - Clerky: formation plus SAFEs and stock issuance — [Flowjam](https://www.flowjam.com/blog/clerky-review-2026-complete-guide-pricing-alternatives)
  - Neither serves restaurants, contractors or freelancers' operational paperwork.

### Inferences
- What incumbents miss, which ShipShape could target:
  1. **A persistent, portable business profile that autofills ANY official PDF**, not just the provider's own templates. Rocket Lawyer and LegalZoom fill their own proprietary forms.
  2. **Sharing and requests between parties.** No profiled competitor offers a shareable "paperwork passport" with two-way document requests: a GC asks a sub for a COI/W-9/license, or a landlord asks a restaurant for permits. Common Paper's sharing is limited to B2B contract negotiation.
  3. **Vertical breadth for non-startups.** Restaurants (health permits, liquor licenses) and contractors (licenses, COIs, lien waivers) are poorly served by startup-focused Atlas and Clerky, and only generically served by LegalZoom and Rocket Lawyer.
  4. **Trust and billing positioning.** No-trap cancellation and no registered-agent lock-in differentiate directly against the top complaint category.
  5. **No-advice positioning** is a regulatory advantage, given the FTC DoNotPay order and LegalZoom's own UPL risk disclosures. ShipShape's AI should "suggest official forms the user accepts" and never "advise".
  6. **Free CC BY templates** (Common Paper, Bonterms) can fill the "commercial contract" slot cheaply.
- Main threat: LegalZoom's license-research AI and compliance monitoring overlap with "suggest official forms". Rocket Lawyer already bundles unlimited docs plus e-sign for $149/yr, which sets a low price anchor.
